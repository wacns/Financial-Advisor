# FinancialAdvisor features

FinancialAdvisor shares React components and financial commands between its React Native Web client and Expo iOS/Android clients. The application does not use a WebView wrapper. This release implements the feature gaps identified from the public FinancialAdvisor reference modules. See [feature coverage and verification](FEATURE-PARITY.md) for what has been exercised and what still needs a device release check.

## Accounts and ledger

Accounts support current/bank accounts, cash, savings, investments, credit cards, fixed deposits, loans and money lent. Add, edit and delete unused accounts; reconcile a signed balance through an explicit ledger adjustment. Accounts with transactions or schedules cannot change currency or be deleted. Loan/card balances owed are entered as negative numbers. Optional APR, credit limit, cost basis, start date, tenure and maturity support utilization, gain, term progress, payment-count and simple-interest estimates. Estimates never accrue or change balances. Linked loan installments transfer principal into the loan account and record interest/fees as a separate expense.

Transactions support income, expenses, transfers and adjustments, with editing, deletion, copying, times, up to five labels, notes and saved templates. Edits reverse the original balance effects and apply the replacement on a clone before saving. Deletes reverse the recorded effects; deleting a debt repayment restores principal. Schedule due dates remain advanced after deletion, avoiding accidental duplicate occurrences. Repayments are replaced through delete and re-record rather than directly edited. Templates and quick entry open a review draft without saving automatically. Search includes names, categories, notes and labels. Filter by account, type and reporting month; export the resulting view as CSV.

Transfers subtract the source amount and credit the actual received amount in the destination currency. If omitted, the destination amount uses saved manual rates. Transfers and reconciliation adjustments stay outside income, spending and budgets. All amounts preserve three-decimal precision with integer-thousandth aggregation. This app records activity manually and never moves money or asks for bank credentials.

## Budgets, goals and salary

Basic category/subcategory budgets repeat monthly or yearly. Setting the same category/period updates its limit; delete removes the tracker only. Goals support names, targets, saved progress, editing and deletion. Goals retain the currency in which they were created; changing reporting currency never relabels their saved amounts.

Cycle budgets support a configurable salary-cycle day (1–31, clamped in shorter months). With day 1, an October cycle is October 1–31. With day 25, October is September 25–October 24. The ending month labels the cycle. Each budget retains its own dates, currency and cycle day after reporting preferences change. Split limits over four weeks, with week four covering remaining days. Add category sub-budgets and include/exclude paths; more specific rules override a parent rule. Saving rejects over-allocation, overlapping child categories, duplicate cycles and overlapping dates. Copy to another cycle, inspect spending/weekly allocations, daily room, pace forecasts, six preceding cycles of spending and accumulated savings from completed budgets. Projections exclude future transactions and do not change balances.

Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly schedules support bills, income, salary and transfers, editing, pause/resume and deletion. Recording confirms one occurrence and advances the original anchor, including month ends/leap years. Schedules never post automatically. The salary workspace records receipts for a selected salary month and assigns existing income without duplicating it. Income assigned to a salary month is included in that monthly report even when received on another date. The commitment forecast averages recurring frequencies into monthly income/outgoings and includes borrowed-debt installments.

Personal debts track money borrowed/lent, person/lender, currency, principal, installment and repayment history. Borrowed repayments spend from the selected account; received repayments of money lent are income. Compare principal with the cash amount converted into debt currency. Adding a tracker does not disburse money.

## Projects, warranties and receipts

Projects support budgets, currency, dates, notes, archive/reopen, category allocations and contingency. Vendor agreements include values, dates, notes and signed variations. Milestones include advance/milestone/retention type, due/completion/paid status and partial payments. Validation keeps allocations within budget and milestone schedules within agreements, and prevents recorded payments from exceeding milestone amounts. Link an existing expense without charging the account again. Unlinking changes project totals only. Preserve history by deleting a project only after explicit confirmation; ledger records remain. Export the project with linked payments and attach/download PDF, text or image documents up to 1 MB each.

Warranties retain item, category, brand, serial, retailer, purchase/expiry dates, notes and receipt images. Search and filter active, expiring within 30 days or expired items. Attach/download receipt photos and include them in backups.

Receipt recognition runs locally: self-hosted Tesseract/English data in the browser, bundled ML Kit Latin recognition on Android and Apple Vision on iOS. Choose an image or use the native camera. Recognized text is editable; parse merchant, total, date, time, category and currency into a review draft. Conflicting totals and ambiguous day/month dates remain blank. Nothing posts until the user reviews and saves. Initial browser OCR downloads assets from this deployment; receipt pixels are not sent to an OCR service. Native camera, photo and OCR behavior still needs physical-device verification.

## Reports, preferences and data

Overview, account reports, category totals, six-month trends and payment calendars share the same ledger. Flexible reports add month/quarter/year/custom date ranges, account scopes and grouping by category, parent, description, account or label combination. Open groups to inspect and edit their transactions. Transfers and adjustments are excluded from financial flow totals. Calendar projections are read-only; only the next scheduled occurrence can be recorded.

Settings provide English/Arabic, RTL layout, light/dark/system themes, motion, reporting cycle, reporting currency and manual exchange rates. Supported currencies: BHD, USD, EUR, GBP, SAR, AED, KWD, OMR, QAR and INR. Rates are BHD per currency unit and defaults are illustrative, not live market quotes. Existing root `currency: BHD` remains a storage compatibility marker; each account/goal/project/budget retains its own currency. Reporting conversion does not rewrite historical amounts. Category management adds, renames and removes unused categories/subcategories while updating linked records.

Full JSON backups include attachments, settings, templates and every collection. Restore validates and previews counts before explicitly replacing the current workspace. Accepts this app's version-2 format, earlier BHD workspace exports and reference `financial-advisor-backup` version 1 (including nested projects and cycle budgets). Reference integer-thousandth fields and decimal goal/basic-budget values are converted separately. CSV imports accept `date,type,amount` plus optional `category,name,note,time,labels`; our exported `Description` and `Amount (currency)` headers are also accepted. A CSV currency must match the selected account; import into a selected account, skip exact duplicates and validate all rows before saving. CSV imports support income and expenses. JSON backups are unencrypted exports; keep them private. Maximum import: 16 MB; CSV: 5,000 rows.

## Native release features

Android Quick Entry widgets show income/expense buttons and up to four template names after opt-in. Balances stay inside the app. Opening a widget verifies its owner and opens a draft; sign-out or disabling clears widget data. iOS home-screen widgets are not provided (the inspected reference exposes Android widgets only).

The native privacy gate uses enrolled device authentication at launch and after a minute away, and covers app content while backgrounded. Screenshot/recording prevention uses the platform API where supported. Native sessions and preferences/demo data use SecureStore; prior AsyncStorage preferences migrate after a successful secure write. Signed-in records persist in the existing per-user Supabase JSON workspace with RLS; this is not an offline cloud-editing/sync engine.

Push remains opt-in per device. Workspace reminder preferences select Bahrain hour, daily/weekly cadence, payment/salary reminders and educational check-ins. Weekly sends are on Sunday. The backend sends generic notifications without financial details and deduplicates successful sends per installation/day. Firebase/APNs credentials, an installed native binary and real-device delivery checks remain necessary; a passing browser or Hermes build does not establish push readiness. See [MOBILE.md](MOBILE.md).

Existing storage/table identifiers (`northline_workspaces`, `northline-bh-demo-v1`) remain for compatibility. Normalization adds empty feature collections to an older workspace without adding sample records to real accounts. The demo has its own storage namespace. Bahrain civil dates remain in Asia/Bahrain while traveling.
# Organization, commitments and collaboration (1.7.0)

Use the sidebar on a wide screen, or **More** on a phone, to reach Shared accounts, Subscriptions, Installments, Payees & payers, Tags, Currency tools and Capture & assistant. The sidebar scrolls when the page list exceeds the available height.

- **Accounts:** choose a color/icon, default entry account, reward rate and whether an account contributes to tracked total balance. Excluding an account preserves its history and balance. Reward rates apply to future expenses, including newly recorded bill payments; they do not retroactively award points to old entries.
- **Transactions:** pin important entries, search contacts/locations/labels and use Advanced filters for dates, amounts and multiple accounts. Pinned entries remain subject to the active filters. Export downloads the filtered results.
- **Payees & payers / Tags:** create reusable contacts and colored tags, then drill into linked history. Renaming a tag updates existing transaction/template labels. Used contacts/tags cannot be deleted without removing their links.
- **Subscriptions:** track renewal intervals and trial end dates. Recording a payment creates a reviewed expense; **Mark paid only** updates tracking without another ledger expense. Use that option when the payment already exists elsewhere. Paused/cancelled subscriptions stop projecting due payments.
- **Installments:** enter a total, count, first date and interval. The final installment absorbs thousandth-unit rounding so the schedule equals the purchase total. The tracker does not create a loan or charge the purchase upfront. Deleting a linked payment reverses its cash effect and reopens that tracker occurrence.
- **Shared accounts:** sign in, create an independent shared ledger and invite an editor or viewer using an expiring single-use code. Owners manage membership; editors change its income/expense records; viewers read/export. Sharing does not reveal your personal ledger or automatically combine balances in personal reports.
- **Capture & assistant:** paste one payment message per line or use speech to prepare drafts. Check every field, then save through the normal entry form. Local insights answer questions about tracked totals without sending data to a model. Cloud AI is optional and needs [server setup](ASSISTANT.md) plus explicit sharing consent.
- **Currency tools:** calculate using saved rates, fetch provider quotes, review their date, and explicitly apply them. Applying rates changes conversions, not stored amounts. ATM search opens Maps with the area you enter.

New collections are empty when an older workspace opens. No automatic subscriptions, contacts, shared records, expenses or balance changes are inserted. Complete JSON backups preserve the new personal metadata; independently shared records remain in their shared ledger and support a separate CSV export.
